ALGORITHM FUNDAMENTALS

What is LBPH Face Recognizer?

LBPH stands for Local Binary Patterns Histograms — an efficient facial feature extraction algorithm.

1. recognizer.train()

Feature Pattern Learning

Accepts cropped face image arrays and corresponding numeric student IDs. Converts pixel grids into mathematical histogram patterns.

recognizer.train(faces, np.array(labels))
2. recognizer.save()

Model Memory Export

Saves the trained histogram weights into face_model.yml on disk so our AI retains memory across sessions.

recognizer.save("face_model.yml")
Why it helps: Allows our camera to recognize students even under varying lighting and expressions!
